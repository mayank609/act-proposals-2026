\begin{center}
{\large\bfseries Annexure I}\\[4pt]
{\bfseries Offline Roll Number Pattern at IIT Jodhpur}
\end{center}
\setlength{\parskip}{3pt}\renewcommand{\rpart}[1]{\par\smallskip\underline{#1}\par}

At IIT Jodhpur, each student is assigned a unique roll number that provides key information about their academic programme and department. The roll number consists of four parts, as described below:

\begin{center}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.1}
\begin{tabular}{cccc}
  {\LARGE\underline{B}} & {\LARGE\underline{24}} & {\LARGE\underline{ME}} & {\LARGE\underline{1072}} \\[2pt]
  \small 1 & \small 2 & \small 3 & \small 4
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\rpart{1. Programme Type:}
The first character of the roll number signifies the type of academic programme the student is enrolled in:
\begin{multicols}{3}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{B:} Bachelor's Programme
  \item \textbf{M:} Master's Programme
  \item \textbf{P:} Ph.D. Programme
\end{itemize}
\end{multicols}
For example, a roll number beginning with ``B'' indicates that the student is pursuing a Bachelor's degree, while ``M'' denotes a Master's student and ``P'' denotes a Ph.D. student.

\rpart{2. Year of Enrolment:}
The second part of the roll number is a two-digit code that represents the year in which the student commenced their degree. These are the last two digits of the corresponding year.

For example, a roll number with its second part as ``24'' indicates that the student enrolled in the year 2024.

\rpart{3. Department Code:}
The third part of the roll number identifies the department the student belongs to. Below is a list of department codes and their corresponding disciplines:
\begin{multicols}{2}\raggedright
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{CS:} Computer Science and Engineering
  \item \textbf{AI:} AI and Data Science (CSE Department)
  \item \textbf{EE:} Electrical Engineering
  \item \textbf{EC:} Electronics Engineering
  \item \textbf{ME:} Mechanical Engineering
  \item \textbf{CH:} Chemical Engineering
  \item \textbf{CI:} Civil Engineering
  \item \textbf{MT:} Materials and Metallurgy
  \item \textbf{BB:} Bioengineering and Biotechnology
  \item \textbf{MA:} Mathematics
  \item \textbf{PH:} Physics
  \item \textbf{CY:} Chemistry
\end{itemize}
\end{multicols}

\rpart{4. Unique Identifier:}
The final part of the roll number is a \textbf{system-generated numeric code} assigned to each student.
\begin{itemize}
  \item For students enrolled before 2023, this identifier is a \textbf{3-digit number} starting with \textbf{001}.
  \item For students enrolled in 2023 and onwards, this identifier is a \textbf{4-digit number} starting with \textbf{1001}.
\end{itemize}

For example:
\begin{itemize}
  \item A student enrolled in 2022 with roll number ``B22ME072'' has a 3-digit unique identifier (072).
  \item A student enrolled in 2024 with roll number ``B24ME1072'' has a 4-digit unique identifier (1072).
\end{itemize}

IIT Jodhpur also accommodates students pursuing their degree in the online mode. However, as a part of the Inter-IIT Tech Meet 15.0, only students pursuing their degrees in the offline mode will be part of the final contingent that will visit IIT Guwahati.
