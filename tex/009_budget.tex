\subhead{Request for advance instead of reimbursement}
Due to the time-sensitive nature of the payments, it is requested that, instead of post-payment and reimbursement, almost the entire budget, i.e.\ \textbf{\rs~7,86,554 out of \rs~8,00,000 (about 98\%)}, be issued as an advance to the accounts of the Faculty Advisors, Inter IIT Tech Meet, as follows:

\textbf{Time sensitivity of the payments:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Registration:} registrations close 10 days after the release of each High Prep PS (tentatively 10th--20th October) and 7 days after the release of Mid, Low and No Prep PS. The fees of each prep category must be paid at one go by the stipulated date, with proof of payment, for the teams to be registered (Rulebook 3.5, 3.7).
  \item \textbf{Travel:} train reservations open 60 days before the journey, and 91 confirmed 3rd AC berths on a weekly train can only be secured if the tickets are booked as soon as the booking opens.
  \item \textbf{Accommodation:} the fees must be paid along with the Bonafide list by 7th December 2026.
  \item \textbf{Students:} an advance avoids the students having to make these payments from their own pockets, as was necessary in the previous edition due to delays in approval.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Settlement:} the advance will be settled by submitting the receipts/invoices from IIT Guwahati and copies of the travel tickets to the concerned office, and any unspent amount will be returned. Invoices for every payment will be maintained and shared with the Faculty Advisors and all relevant offices.

We request the approval of the budget and the release of the advance at the earliest, so that the registrations, payments and travel bookings can be completed within the deadlines and IIT Jodhpur can deliver its best performance in the Inter IIT Tech Meet 15.0.

\subhead{Team selection}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Each Problem Statement will be floated among the students as soon as it is released, and teams will be formed from the responses received.
  \item The teams will be evaluated by the Faculty Advisors, Inter IIT Tech Meet and the Associate Dean (Student Affairs), and the final team will be recommended.
  \item The selection follows Appendix 1-B of the Students' Gymkhana Constitution (Annexure 2-B): the Contingent Leader is selected by the GS and Faculty Advisors with DoSA approval; the Deputy Contingent Leaders and Domain Leads by the GS and Contingent Leader with Faculty Advisor approval; and the contingent members by the CL, DCLs and Domain Leads with ADSA approval.
  \item The teams will be registered within 10 days of release for High Prep and 7 days for Mid, Low and No Prep PS (Rulebook 3.5 (4)), and the registration fees will be paid from the advance at the time of registration.
\end{itemize}

\subannex{1-C :}{Travel and accommodation (Rulebook 3.2)}

The travel window is 8th--10th December 2026, and accommodation at IIT Guwahati is provided from 08:00 hrs on 10th December. Contingents arriving between midnight and 08:00 hrs on 10th December incur no additional charges, subject to informing the Organising Committee in advance. The proposed route is:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{|L{3.4cm}|L{4.4cm}|L{2.8cm}|L{2.8cm}|}
\hline
\textbf{Leg} & \textbf{Train} & \textbf{Departure} & \textbf{Arrival} \\ \hline
Jodhpur -- Jaipur & 22978 JU JP Express & Mon 7 Dec, 16:00 & Mon 7 Dec, 21:15 \\ \hline
Jaipur -- Kamakhya & 19615 Kavi Guru Express & Mon 7 Dec, 23:10 & Thu 10 Dec, 00:10 \\ \hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\subhead{Justification for the non-direct route}
\begin{itemize}
  \item The direct 15631 Barmer--Guwahati Express reaches Guwahati on 8th December night, 1.5 days before the accommodation begins, requiring an extended stay at \rs~600 (+ 5\% GST) per person per day (about \rs~1.15 lakh for the contingent).
  \item The direct 15623 Bhagat Ki Kothi--Kamakhya Express reaches Kamakhya at 22:00 on 10th December, after the Opening Ceremony.
  \item The route via Jaipur reaches within the no-charge arrival window on 10th December, with a sufficient buffer before the Opening Ceremony and at a lower fare.
\end{itemize}

A faculty advisor is requested to accompany the contingent to IIT Guwahati. The students will arrange their return journey after the event.

\subannex{1-D :}{Registration fees and payment (Rulebook 3.6, 3.7)}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Registration fee: \rs~800 (+ 18\% GST) for every participant, including the admin team and members of SAC and EC (Rulebook 3.6).
  \item Food and accommodation: \rs~3,000 (+ 5\% GST) per offline attendee (Rulebook 3.6.1).
  \item Payments are to be made through the institute portal or fund transfer, at one go for each prep category, and the proof of payment is to be submitted to the Organising Committee (Rulebook 3.7). These payments will be made from the advance requested to the Faculty Advisors' accounts.
\end{itemize}
