\subhead{Justification for the proposed route}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Direct trains are not available for the contingent: the only direct train from Jodhpur in the travel window, the 15623 Bhagat Ki Kothi--Kamakhya Express, reaches Kamakhya at 22:00 on 10th December, after the Opening Ceremony, with only 60 3A berths available, and the other direct train, the 15631 Barmer--Guwahati Express, reaches 1.5 days before the accommodation begins.
  \item The non-Rajdhani trains from Delhi are also not available: the North East Express has no confirmed berths (3A on RAC), and the Brahmaputra Mail has only 29 3A berths, far short of the 91 needed, with an unsafe 75-minute connection across Delhi stations. The 12424 Rajdhani Express is already waitlisted.
  \item The 20504 Rajdhani Express is therefore the only option with confirmed berths for the full contingent. It reaches Guwahati on 10th December, within the travel window and without additional accommodation charges, and its fare includes meals for the 31-hour journey.
  \item The Rajdhani fare is dynamic and rises as berths are sold; it rose from \rs~3,685 to \rs~4,135 within a week. It is therefore budgeted at \rs~5,000 per person, so that the difference serves as a contingency against further increases until all 91 tickets are booked.
  \item For the Jodhpur -- Delhi leg, the 22481 Express has the most available berths (199) and arrives at 05:20, leaving a buffer of six hours before the Rajdhani departs. The 22491 Mandore Express (124 berths, \rs~1,020, arriving 06:45 at Delhi Jn.) and the 15623 Express (130 berths, \rs~985, arriving 04:40 at Delhi Jn.) are alternatives.
  \item For the journey from the campus to Jodhpur railway station, the institute bus will be requested first; only if it is not available will a bus be hired from outside (\rs~12,000 budgeted).
\end{itemize}

Travel is requested only up to Guwahati; the students will bear their return journey to the campus or home after the event.
