{\bfseries Subject: Proposal for ACT Council budget for the AY 2026-27}

Respected Ma'am,

This proposal details the overall budgetary plans for various technical societies under the Academic, Career \& Technology (ACT) Council, as well as allied events and common requirements under the purview of the ACT Council, for the Academic Year 2026-27.

The {\bfseries 9 technical societies} are:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Robotics Society
  \item RAID (AI Society)
  \item DevlUp Labs (Development Society)
  \item Product Society
  \item Boltheads (Automobile Society)
  \item Quant Society
  \item PSOC (Programming Society)
  \item Nexus (Astronomy Society)
  \item Creara (Game Development Society)
\end{enumerate}

Apart from the 9 technical societies, the {\bfseries Career Development Cell (CDC)} and {\bfseries Academics} also fall under the ACT Council.

The overall budget summary is given below, followed by the individual Society details and their expenditure plans for the AY 2026-27. I request your approval for the overall ACT Council budget breakdown, so as to begin the actual procurements at the earliest.
