{\bfseries Subject: Proposal for drinking water campers in the Tinkering Lab for Inter IIT Tech Meet 15.0}

Respected Ma'am,

Students preparing for the Inter IIT Tech Meet 15.0 will be working for long hours in the Tinkering Lab from 10th October 2026 to 10th December 2026. At present, there is no drinking water facility in the lab, and students have to leave their work to fetch water. To make this easier, we propose to place 3 drinking water campers (20 L) in the Tinkering Lab, with daily refills by the vendor, for the full preparation period.
