{\bfseries Subject: Request for access to institute HPC facilities for teams in Inter IIT}

Respected Sir,

This proposal requests access to the High Performance Computing (HPC) facilities of the institute for the teams of IIT Jodhpur participating in Inter IIT, under the purview of the Academic, Career \& Technology (ACT) Council, from \textbf{10th October, 2026 to 15th December, 2026}, covering the preparation period leading up to the Meet.

Several problem statements for the teams in Inter IIT involve computationally intensive tasks such as training and fine-tuning machine learning and deep learning models, processing large datasets, and running simulations. These tasks require GPU and high-memory compute resources that cannot be handled on personal laptops or standard lab systems. At present, the teams do not have access to any such computing resources, which severely limits their ability to develop, test and iterate on their solutions within the given timelines.

Access to the institute HPC facilities will enable the teams to work on these problem statements effectively, produce competitive solutions, and represent IIT Jodhpur strongly at the Meet.

On behalf of the ACT Council, we assure the following:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Access will be given only to the nominated domain leaders of the teams in Inter IIT, as listed in Annexure I.
  \item The resources will be used solely for Inter IIT problem statements and for no other purpose.
  \item All HPC usage policies, job scheduling rules and resource limits of the institute will be followed, subject to communication of SOP by the competent authority.
  \item The nominated domain leaders will be responsible for the usage of their accounts and for communication with the HPC administrators.
  \item The access will be discontinued after 15th December, 2026.
\end{itemize}

We therefore kindly request you to grant permission and facilitate access to the institute HPC facilities for the nominated domain leaders of the teams in Inter IIT, subject to kind approval and proper communication by the competent authority. Your support will be of great help to the teams in their preparation for the Meet.
